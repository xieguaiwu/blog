A comprehensive close reading of Abe Kōbō's 1973 novel *The Box Man* (*Hako Otoko*), tracing its metamorphosis-and-transformation motif back to his 1949 debut *Kani* ("Crab Cactus"), mapping the novel's intricate structure of 22 chapters plus two newspaper clippings, and analyzing its shifting narrators, unreliable narration, and layered fictional worlds as a meditation on identity, voyeurism, and the "pseudo-identity" of modern existence.
